- License plates: white rectangular, black text, no EU strip. Format: А123ВС 77 (letter-digits-letters + region code).
- Cyrillic script. Key letters: Ы, Э, Ё, Ъ. улица/ул. (street), город/г. (city), область (region).
- Utility poles: A-frame wooden poles common in rural areas; concrete cylindrical poles; bases often painted BLACK. Massive overhead wire clusters.
- Bollards: thin post + black top + red vertical reflector stripe. Often at abnormally wide intersections.
- Road lines: white center dashed; white outer; road quality degrades rapidly outside major cities.
- Architecture: Soviet khrushchyovka grey concrete blocks in towns; wooden izba houses with carved window frames in rural areas.
