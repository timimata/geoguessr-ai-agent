- Left-hand traffic. YELLOW outer road lines (unique in Africa — confirm immediately vs other African countries).
- Unique triple center lines: 2 solid white + 1 dashed, or 2 white + 1 yellow on main roads.
- "Bird poles": concrete poles with 1–5 horizontal bars + thin white insulators resembling perched birds. Extremely distinctive.
- Green directional signs with yellow road numbers (N1, R27 etc.) — N-roads and R-roads exclusive to SA.
- License plates: white background, black text, green left strip with flag colors; rectangular format.
- Language: Afrikaans + English. Look for "STOP / HALT", "PADWERK" (roadworks), "UITGANG" (exit).
- Landscape: Cape Town area (lat -34, Mediterranean fynbos shrubs), Garden Route (lat -33, green coastal), Karoo (lat -32 to -31, dry semi-desert plateau), Highveld/Gauteng (lat -26, flat golden grassland, Joburg), KwaZulu-Natal (lat -29, subtropical, Durban), Limpopo (lat -24, dry acacia savanna).
- CONFUSION with Namibia/Botswana: those lack bird poles and green N-road signs; SA has better roads. Australia also left-hand but WHITE outer lines.
- CONFUSION with Spain/Europe: SA has yellow outer lines + left-hand drive — no European country drives on left. Bird poles are unique.
- REGIONAL COORDS: Cape Town (-33.9,18.4), Garden Route (-33.5,22), Karoo (-32,24), Joburg (-26.2,28.0), Durban (-29.9,31.0), Limpopo (-23.9,29.5).
