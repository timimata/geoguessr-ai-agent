- License plates: yellow background with black text (rear yellow, front white). Format includes letters+numbers; text in Hebrew and Latin.
- Language: Hebrew script (right-to-left, distinctive blocky angular letters). Arabic bilingual on many road signs. Latin transliterations also present.
- Utility poles: concrete poles with multiple horizontal crossbars; often with transformer boxes; wooden poles less common.
- Road lines: yellow center lines (dashed); white outer lines. Road signs in Hebrew, Arabic, and English.
- Landscape: Mediterranean coast (flat, urban), Negev desert (south, lat 30-29, brown arid), Galilee (north, green hills), Jordan Valley (east, very low altitude, Dead Sea area).
- Architecture: Jerusalem stone (cream/honey limestone) in Jerusalem area; modern concrete in Tel Aviv; Arab villages have flat-roofed stone houses.
- Unique: road signs tri-lingual (Hebrew top, Arabic middle, English bottom). Highway numbers in diamonds.
- CONFUSION: Israel↔Tunisia/Morocco — Arabic script alone ≠ Israel; Hebrew confirms Israel. Israel↔Jordan — Jordan has white plates with black/red text; no Hebrew.
- REGIONAL COORDS: Tel Aviv (32.1,34.8), Jerusalem (31.8,35.2), Haifa north (32.8,35.0), Negev desert center (30.5,34.8), Galilee (32.9,35.5).
