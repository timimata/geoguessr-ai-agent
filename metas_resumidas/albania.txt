- License plates: Blue strips on both sides OR single red strip on left (unique in Europe).
- Bollards: Black top, red/grey reflector, white base.
- Utility poles: Distinctive upside-down triangle tops or simple horizontal bars.
- Road signs: Backs painted black; stop sign "S" has short, angled ends; speed limit numbers in bold font.
- Chevrons: White on black.
- Guardrails: Flat, wide endings that bend away from the road.
- Architecture: Pastel-colored blocky buildings, orange tiled roofs, numerous balconies, and cylindrical roof water tanks.
- Language: Uses Ë/ë and Ç/ç; street sign reads "rruga".
