- License plates: white with blue EU left strip (GR) and white background. Black text, standard EU rectangular format.
- Language: Greek alphabet (unique curvy letters: α, β, γ, δ, ε, ζ, θ, λ, μ, ξ, π, σ, φ, ψ, ω). Street = "ΟΔΟΣ". Many signs bilingual Greek+Latin.
- Utility poles: wooden poles with crossbar; concrete poles with multiple crossbars; often tilted/leaning in rural areas.
- Road lines: white outer lines; white dashed center; road quality varies greatly (good in Attica, poor in rural).
- Landscape: dry rocky Mediterranean — scrubby hillsides (maquis/phrygana), olive groves, white-washed cubic houses (islands), terracotta roofs on mainland.
- Architecture: white cubic houses with blue doors/windows (Cyclades islands); terracotta roof tiles (mainland/Crete); exposed concrete common in modern builds.
- Bollards: white with single orange reflector band (similar to EU standard but often tilted/damaged).
- CONFUSION: Greece↔Serbia/Bulgaria — confirm with Greek alphabet or GR plate. Greece↔Turkey — Turkey uses Latin alphabet after 1928; Turkish signs have Ğ, İ, Ö, Ş, Ü. Islands (lat 37-38, lon 23-26 Aegean; lat 35 Crete).
- REGIONAL COORDS: Athens (37.9,23.7), Thessaloniki (40.6,22.9), Crete (35.3,25.1), Peloponnese (37.5,22.3), islands lon 23-26.
