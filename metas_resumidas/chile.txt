- Long narrow country (4300km N-S); Andes ALWAYS to the east, Pacific to the west.
- License plates: white standard; orange for commercial/taxi. No EU-style strip.
- Utility poles: square concrete with side indentations, few horizontal crossbars, small upper pinholes; wooden poles also common.
- Road lines: exclusively all-white OR all-yellow (yellow = national parks/high snowfall; essentially unique to Chile in Americas).
- Bollards: Spanish-style white front + yellow-orange rear reflector. Bridges often have red-painted fence posts.
- Spanish text on all signs. Mapuche (Mapudungun) bilingual signs in south.
- REGIONAL GUIDE: Atacama north (lat -18 to -27): hyper-arid, bare brown/red rock, zero vegetation. Central valley/Santiago (lat -33): semi-arid hills, vineyards, eucalyptus. Bio-Bio/Araucanía (lat -37 to -39): Araucaria (monkey-puzzle) trees — distinctive umbrella crown, unique to Chile/Argentina. Lake District (lat -40 to -43): lush green, snow-capped volcanoes, looks like NZ/Switzerland. Patagonia (lat -45+): barren steppe, looks like Montana but Spanish signs.
- CONFUSION: Chile→Romania/Ukraine (both have similar color palette + narrow roads) — confirm with Andes presence or Spanish text. Atacama→Middle East — Pacific cliffs visible to west confirm Chile.
