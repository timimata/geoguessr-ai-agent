- Focus on what's instantly visible without moving/zooming.
- *Alphabet/Text:* Signs feature dual Cyrillic + Latin text (two distinct lines). Look for letter “Ъ” or “ул.” (ul.).
- *Utility Poles:* Cylindrical concrete poles with distinctive tops: 3 large, alternating hook-shaped insulators (facing up or down).
- *Road Markings:* Thin white roadlines. Poor road surface (frequent cracks/potholes).
