- Left-hand traffic (British legacy). Dense urban high-rises — one of the world's most vertical skylines.
- License plates: white background with black text. Format: 2 letters + 4 digits (AB1234). No country code strip.
- Language: Traditional Chinese + English bilingual on ALL signs. Cantonese is the spoken language. Traditional characters (not simplified like mainland China).
- Road lines: yellow outer lines (left-side driving); white center lines.
- Landscape: extremely dense urban canyon (HK Island/Kowloon), surrounded by green hills. Victoria Harbour between HK Island and Kowloon. New Territories more suburban/rural with mountains.
- Architecture: dense residential towers in rows, often identical blocks. British colonial government buildings in Central. Trams on Hong Kong Island (unique double-decker trams).
- Infrastructure: red taxis (urban), green taxis (NT), blue taxis (Lantau); double-decker buses (like UK); MTR metro stations.
- CONFUSION with mainland China: HK has Traditional Chinese + English; China has Simplified Chinese only. HK drives on LEFT; mainland drives on RIGHT. HK = extremely dense vertical city.
- COORDS: Hong Kong Island/Central (22.3,114.2), Kowloon (22.3,114.2), New Territories (22.4,114.1).
