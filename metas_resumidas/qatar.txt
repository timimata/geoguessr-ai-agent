- Long white license plates with maroon left strip (cars); square black (trucks); blue (police).
- Solid white outer road lines; white or yellow center lines (rare yellow outer).
- Round utility poles with 3 top insulators (middle highest) + crossbar with 2 diagonal supports.
- White-on-red/blue chevron signs; brown ground-mounted street signs (Education City).
- Crossing signs depict man in traditional thobe.
- Predominantly flat, arid desert landscape with sparse vegetation; coastal shrubs near Fuwayrit.
- Key landmarks: Al Bayt Stadium (black/white stripes, tent roof), Lusail (golden bowl + screw towers), Education City (black-tiled roads, grey diamond-clad buildings), Khalifa Stadium (dual arches), Torch Tower (hyperboloid).
- Street signs display municipality/zone numbers.
