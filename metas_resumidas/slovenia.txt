- *Landscape:* Hilly/forested nationwide; flat ag fields east; alpine NW; Mediterranean SW coast.
- *Architecture:* Alpine style: light walls, dark wood trim, half-hipped roofs. Look for *kozolec* (slatted wooden hay sheds).
- *Alphabet/Language:* Slovene uses Č, Š, Ž. Italian bilingual signs appear in western coastal region.
- *Bollards:* White base, black cap. Bright red front reflector, white back reflector.
- *Road Markings:* Long outer dashed lines are highly characteristic.
