- Landscape: Jagged, steep green mountains rising directly from wide fjords; virtually no natural trees.
- Architecture: Wooden houses with painted above-ground stone basements; roofs are metal or turf/grass.
- Road Infrastructure: Guardrails feature unique yellow reflectors. Directional signs are yellow with thin black borders & metal frames.
- Bollards: Small wooden posts, typically yellow-topped (sometimes red).
- Language: Faroese alphabet includes Á, Ð, Í, Ó, Ú, Ý, Æ, Ø (distinguishes from Icelandic É/Þ).
- Vegetation: Lush, vibrant green grass/moss covering slopes; completely treeless natural landscape.
