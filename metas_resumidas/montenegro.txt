- *License Plates:* EU blue strip + red coat-of-arms circle (visible through blur); white plates = Albania.
- *Bollards:* White posts, black caps, red front/white back reflectors (also Slovenia).
- *Road Markings:* Black-on-white or red-on-yellow chevrons; yellow outer lines on southern E80.
- *Guardrails:* Frequently rusted; mix of A-type and B-type common.
- *Utility Poles:* Simple round concrete or wooden poles.
