- License plates: white background, black text. Format: 3 letters + 4 digits (old) or newer format varies.
- Language: Malagasy + French bilingual. Malagasy is a unique Austronesian language (related to Malay, not African!). Key words: "lalana" (road), "tanàna" (town), "Araben'" (avenue). Also French: "route", "rue".
- Landscape: east coast (humid tropical jungle, escarpment dropping to sea); central high plateau (cooler, 1200-1800m, terraced rice paddies, red laterite soil); west coast (dry deciduous forest, baobab trees); south (spiny desert, cactus-like plants). Red/orange laterite soil almost everywhere.
- Vegetation: BAOBAB TREES — massive bottle-shaped trunks with sparse branches at top, unique to Madagascar/Africa. Also traveller's palms (fan-shaped).
- Road quality: very poor. Mostly unpaved or badly maintained tarmac. Zebu (humped cattle) on roads.
- Architecture: traditional Malagasy houses — two-story red brick with wooden balconies; steep pitched roofs. Distinctive from other African countries.
- REGIONAL COORDS: Antananarivo/capital (lat -18.9, lon 47.5), east coast Toamasina (-18.1,49.4), west Morondava/baobabs (-20.3,44.3), south Tulear (-23.4,43.7).
