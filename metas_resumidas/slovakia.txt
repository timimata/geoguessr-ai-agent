- Bollards: Orange double reflectors (front), single white (back).
- Guardrails: Wide, unique Type B design.
- Utility poles: Paired, wide round concrete poles. Pole tops: short metal bars, inverted triangle supports, or trident shapes.
- Signage/Language: Blue directional signs with small white arrows. Look for Slovak letters: Ľ, Ô, Ä, Ŕ, Ĺ (vs Czech Ř, Ě, Ů).
