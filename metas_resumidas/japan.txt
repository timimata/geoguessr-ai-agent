- Left-hand traffic. License plates: white (private) or yellow (kei cars, <660cc) background, green text border. Hiragana + numbers on plate.
- Camera: noticeably LOW angle compared to standard Google car. Small camera = lower perspective, closer to road surface.
- Script: Japanese — mixture of Hiragana (rounded cursive), Katakana (angular cursive), Kanji (Chinese-origin complex characters). All three often on same sign.
- Utility poles: wooden poles painted with distinctive YELLOW AND BLACK DIAGONAL STRIPES near the top. Also white insulators in rows. Very dense wiring.
- Road lines: white outer lines; white dashed center OR yellow center (indicating no-overtake). Vending machines ("自販機") visible roadside — ubiquitous in Japan.
- Bollards: distinctive small round-top yellow or white bollards; often with cat-eye reflectors. Guardrails with yellow chevron patterns.
- Curve mirrors: round convex mirrors at almost every rural intersection — very common in Japan.
- Architecture: low-rise residential with tiled hip roofs (gray ceramic tiles); traditional wooden buildings; sliding doors; small garden walls. Modern: glass/concrete urban.
- CONFUSION with South Korea: Korea uses Hangul only (blocky square characters). Japan uses Kanji+Hiragana+Katakana mix. Low camera angle confirms Japan.
- REGIONAL COORDS: Tokyo/Kanto (35.7,139.7), Osaka/Kansai (34.7,135.5), Hokkaido north (43.1,141.3), Kyushu south (32.8,130.7), Okinawa (26.2,127.7), Tohoku northeast (38.7,141.1).
