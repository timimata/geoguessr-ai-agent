- License plates: white with NO EU blue strip (Switzerland is not EU). Black text. Canton code on white oval sticker separate from plate. Format: canton code + letters + numbers.
- Language: quadrilingual — German (northeast, 63%), French (west, 23%), Italian (south Ticino, 8%), Romansh (SE, 1%). Signs match regional language.
- Road lines: white outer lines; white dashed center. Excellent road quality throughout. Mountain tunnels have orange lighting.
- Landscape: Alps dominate southern half; Mittelland (central plateau, flat/hilly); Jura mountains (northwest). Extremely clean, well-maintained infrastructure.
- Architecture: wooden chalets with wide overhanging roofs in alpine areas; half-timber (Fachwerk) in German parts; French-style in west; Italian-style in Ticino. Very well-maintained buildings.
- Bollards: white rounded top with yellow or orange reflector (similar to Germany/Austria but CH plates distinguish).
- CONFUSION with Austria: Austria has AT EU blue strip; Switzerland has plain white NO EU strip. Austria = "EINBAHN" one-way signs; Switzerland = "EINBAHNSTRASSE" (same as Germany) or French "sens unique".
- CONFUSION with France: French plates have blue EU strip with FR; Swiss plates are plain white no strip.
- REGIONAL COORDS: Zurich/German (47.4,8.5), Geneva/French (46.2,6.1), Bern/capital (46.9,7.4), Basel NW (47.6,7.6), Lucerne center (47.1,8.3), Lugano/Italian Ticino (46.0,9.0), Graubünden SE (46.7,9.5).
