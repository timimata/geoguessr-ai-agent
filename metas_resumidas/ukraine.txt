- Long white license plates with a vertical blue (top) & yellow (bottom) strip on the left.
- Cyrillic street signs: look for unique letter Є/є (not Russian Э/э); "street" = вулиця (abbr. вул.).
- Poor road quality: heavily cracked, potholed, and warped pavement, especially in rural areas.
- Streetside infrastructure (fences, bollards, utility boxes) frequently painted blue & yellow.
- Absence of Russian-specific visual markers (e.g., fully white plates, Russian-only Cyrillic).
