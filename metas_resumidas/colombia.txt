- License plates: yellow background with black text (unique in South America alongside Ecuador). Older plates have white background.
- Road backs: distinctive BLACK-AND-WHITE STRIPED CROSS painted on the back of road signs — essentially unique to Colombia.
- Utility poles: wooden poles with single crossbar; concrete poles with horizontal rungs; messy wiring typical.
- Road lines: thin yellow center line (dashed or solid); thin white outer lines.
- Language: Spanish; road signs often show "PARE" (stop), "CEDA EL PASO" (yield), "VÍA" prefix on road names.
- Landscape: extremely varied — Andes run N-S with coffee region (green steep mountains, lat 4-6°N), Caribbean coast (flat, hot), Amazon lowland (east), Pacific coast (jungle).
- Architecture: colorful painted concrete houses; red brick; adobe in rural Andes; corrugated metal roofs.
- CONFUSION with Ecuador: Ecuador uses YELLOW plates (similar to Colombia) but lacks the back-of-sign stripe pattern. Ecuador terrain is more compact/narrower east-west with stronger Andean presence. Colombian coffee region has very lush steep green valleys.
- REGIONAL COORDS: Bogotá (4.7,-74.1), Medellín coffee (6.2,-75.6), Caribbean coast (10.4,-75.5), Cali SW (3.4,-76.5).
