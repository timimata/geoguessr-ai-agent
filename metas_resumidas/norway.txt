- License plates: white with blue EU-style left strip (N). Black text. Standard EU rectangular.
- Road lines: YELLOW center lines (unique in Scandinavia — Sweden and Finland use white center lines). White outer lines.
- Landscape: dramatic fjords (deep coastal inlets with vertical cliffs, lat 60-62°N); mountains (inland); pine/birch forests; coastal archipelagos. Very rugged terrain. Snow visible much of year at altitude.
- Utility poles: wooden poles with crossbar; well-maintained. Often blends into forested backdrop.
- Bollards: white rounded top with red reflector band; red delineator posts on roadsides (common on mountain roads).
- Road signs: blue tunnel signs (Norway has many tunnels — "Tunnel" text on signs). Speed limits in km/h.
- Language: Norwegian (two forms: Bokmål and Nynorsk). "Vei/vg" (road), "gate" (street), "fjord" (inlet). Letters: æ, ø, å.
- CONFUSION with Sweden: Norway has YELLOW center lines; Sweden has WHITE. Norway = fjords + dramatic cliffs; Sweden = flatter forests.
- CONFUSION with Finland: Finland uses white center lines; Norway uses yellow. Finnish landscape flatter.
- REGIONAL COORDS: Oslo (59.9,10.7), Bergen/Fjords (60.4,5.3), Trondheim (63.4,10.4), Tromsø/Arctic (69.6,18.9), Stavanger/Rogaland SW (58.9,5.7), Lofoten NW (68.1,13.6).
