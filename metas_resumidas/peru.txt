- License plates: white background, black text, red letters on left for region code. Older plates: white/black or yellow.
- Language: Spanish. "Jiron/Jr." (street in cities), "Carretera" (highway), "Avenida/Av.". Also Quechua bilingual in highland areas.
- Landscape: three distinct zones — Costa (western coastal desert, lat -5 to -18, extreme arid brown desert strip along Pacific, very dry), Sierra (Andes mountains, lat -9 to -16, altitude 2000-5000m, green terraces, adobe villages), Selva (Amazon jungle east, lat -4 to -12, dense tropical).
- Road quality: poor to moderate. Andean roads extremely winding/narrow. Coastal Pan-American Highway better.
- Utility poles: wooden poles; concrete poles; chaotic wiring. Often painted with political graffiti.
- Architecture: adobe mud-brick houses with straw/tile roofs in highlands; corrugated metal roofs; pastel concrete in cities.
- Vegetation: coastal = zero vegetation (bare desert); sierra = terrace agriculture, eucalyptus, quinoa; selva = tropical jungle.
- CONFUSION with Bolivia: Bolivia is landlocked (no Pacific coast desert). Peru has the extreme coastal desert (Atacama continues into southern Peru). Bolivia has altiplano (flat highland plateau); Peru has more dramatic Andean valleys.
- CONFUSION with Chile: Chile is narrower, better roads, more European-looking. Peru = more rustic, Spanish+Quechua signs, adobe construction.
- REGIONAL COORDS: Lima/coast (-12.0,-77.0), Cusco/highland (-13.5,-71.9), Machu Picchu (-13.2,-72.5), Arequipa (-16.4,-71.5), Iquitos/Amazon (-3.7,-73.3).
