- Virtually no roads — almost all coverage is in towns (Nuuk, Ilulissat, Sisimiut, Qaqortoq). No highway Street View.
- Language: Greenlandic (Kalaallisut) — very long compound words with double consonants (e.g. "Nuuk", "Ilulissat", "Qeqqata"). Danish also present on signs.
- Architecture: brightly colored wooden houses on rock (characteristic Arctic style) — reds, blues, greens, yellows. Pitched roofs. No trees, only rock and tundra.
- Landscape: bare Arctic rock, snow, sea ice, fjords. No vegetation except low tundra plants. Icebergs in sea.
- Roads: short gravel/dirt tracks between buildings. No paved highways. Cars are present in towns.
- Infrastructure: Danish-style street furniture but with Greenlandic names. Danish flag (dannebrog) and Greenlandic flag (Erfalasorput — white+red semicircles) often visible.
- Essentially: if you see colorful wooden houses on bare Arctic rock with Greenlandic text and no trees = Greenland. No other country looks like this.
- COORDS: Nuuk (64.2,-51.7), Ilulissat west (69.2,-51.1), Sisimiut (66.9,-53.7), Qaqortoq south (60.7,-46.0).
