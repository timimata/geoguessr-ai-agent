- License plates: white with blue EU-style left strip (NMK or MK). Black text. Standard rectangular.
- Language: Macedonian (Cyrillic script). Very similar to Bulgarian and Serbian Cyrillic. Unique letters: Ѓ, Ѕ, Ќ, Љ, Њ, Џ. Road signs often bilingual Macedonian + Albanian (in western areas with Albanian minority).
- Landscape: mountainous with valleys; Mediterranean influence in south (Lake Ohrid lat 41, Lake Prespa); continental inland. Rivers: Vardar (central), Treska. Mostly hilly/mountainous terrain.
- Road quality: moderate — main highways decent, rural roads rough. EU-funded improvements ongoing.
- Architecture: Ottoman-era stone buildings in Skopje old bazaar; Byzantine churches; communist concrete blocks; traditional whitewashed stone houses in villages.
- CONFUSION with Serbia/Bulgaria: All three use Cyrillic. North Macedonia unique letters (Ѕ, Ѓ). Bulgaria has BG plates. Serbia has SRB plates. Western NM has Albanian-language signs alongside Macedonian.
- CONFUSION with Albania: Albania uses Latin alphabet (Shqip language). NM = Cyrillic primary.
- COORDS: Skopje (42.0,21.4), Ohrid SW (41.1,20.8), Bitola south (41.0,21.3), Tetovo NW (42.0,20.8).
