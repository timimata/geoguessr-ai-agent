- Right-hand traffic (unlike Philippines which is also right-hand). License plates: black background with white text (unique in SE Asia — Philippines uses white plates).
- Language: Bahasa Indonesia (Latin alphabet). Key words: "Jalan/Jl." (street/road), "Desa" (village), "Kabupaten" (district). No diacritics except occasional ê.
- Utility poles: concrete poles with multiple horizontal rungs (ladder-style); extremely chaotic overhead wiring in cities. Wooden poles in rural areas.
- Road lines: white outer lines; yellow center lines on main roads. Road quality ranges from excellent (Java highways) to poor dirt tracks (Kalimantan/Papua).
- Landscape: tropical — Bali (rice terraces, Hindu temples, lat -8), Java (dense urban, volcanoes, lat -7), Sumatra (jungle, palm oil, lat 0), Kalimantan/Borneo (dense jungle, red soil, lat 0 to +2), Sulawesi (mountainous, lat -2).
- Architecture: traditional Joglo/Limasan wooden houses with curved roofs in Java; Balinese Hindu temples (tiered stone towers); modern concrete in cities.
- CONFUSION with Philippines: Indonesia has BLACK plates; Philippines has WHITE plates. Indonesia = Bahasa Indonesia (no Tagalog). Philippines = left-hand drive no wait — Philippines is RIGHT-hand too. Key: Indonesia black plates vs Philippines white plates.
- CONFUSION with Malaysia: Malaysia has white plates; Bahasa Malaysia very similar to Indonesian but "jalan" is same in both.
- REGIONAL COORDS: Java/Jakarta (-6.2,106.8), Bali (-8.4,115.2), Sumatra Medan (3.6,98.7), Kalimantan Pontianak (0.0,109.3), Sulawesi Makassar (-5.1,119.4).
