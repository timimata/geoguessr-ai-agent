- White license plates for private vehicles, yellow for commercial.
- Road markings are always white; many paved roads lack lines entirely.
- Wooden utility poles with 3 insulators mounted on a metallic crossbar.
- Tropical vegetation dominates south/coast (dense palms); dry savannah & less dense flora north.
- Dense forests concentrated in southwest & eastern regions.
- Buildings/signs frequently feature national flag colors: red, yellow, green, black star.
- Urban signposts are black & white striped; rural ones are unpainted metal.
