- Left-hand traffic (British legacy). License plates: white background, black text, with "UG" code or Ugandan flag element.
- Language: English official + Luganda (Bantu language) + many tribal languages. Road signs in English. "Murram road" = unpaved laterite road.
- Landscape: equatorial green (lat 0 to 4°N) — lush vegetation, banana/matoke plantations everywhere, rolling hills, red laterite soil. Lake Victoria in south. Mountains in west (Rwenzoris). Very green year-round.
- Road quality: mostly poor/unpaved outside Kampala. Red earth roads (murram) with deep ruts.
- Utility poles: wooden poles with crossbar; sometimes concrete. Chaotic wiring in towns.
- Architecture: mud-brick houses with corrugated metal roofs (most rural); banana leaf thatching; concrete block buildings in towns. "Boda-boda" motorcycle taxis everywhere.
- CONFUSION with Kenya: Kenya also left-hand + English + red soil but Kenya has different plate format and more Swahili (Kenya = "barabara" for road). Uganda greener and more banana plantations.
- CONFUSION with Rwanda: Rwanda is more hilly/mountainous; Uganda flatter relative to Rwanda's extreme terracing. Both have red soil and green vegetation.
- REGIONAL COORDS: Kampala (0.3,32.6), Jinja east (0.4,33.2), Mbarara SW (-0.6,30.7), Gulu north (2.8,32.3), Fort Portal west (0.7,30.3).
