- *License plates:* Long white plates, blue EU strip left.
- *Bollards:* Round baton shape, rectangular front reflector, two circular rear reflectors (white/orange).
- *Utility poles:* Inconsistent; mostly round wooden poles with metal caps & diagonal supports, or square concrete poles. Trident pole tops common (1 top, 2 side insulators).
- *Road markings:* Outer roads may use short dashes. Red chevrons with white arrows.
