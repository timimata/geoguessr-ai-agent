- License plates: All-white background, blue text, NO left-side blue EU strip.
- Bollards: Bright yellow with white reflectors (ubiquitous on rural roads).
- Road markings: Strictly all-white lines (solid or dashed); zero yellow lines.
- Directional signs: Yellow background, white inset box for route numbers (dashed box border = nearby route).
