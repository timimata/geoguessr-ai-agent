- License plates: white with blue EU left strip (I). Black text. Rectangular EU format. Older plates (pre-1994) had black background with white text — still visible.
- Road sign backs: DARK GREY/BLACK backs on road signs (unique in western Europe — France/Spain/Germany have lighter backs).
- Language: Italian. "Via" (street), "Corso" (boulevard), "Piazza" (square). Key letters: à, è, é, ì, ò, ù. No W,K,X,Y in native words (foreign loan words only).
- Utility poles: wooden poles with crossbar; concrete poles. Often with ORANGE/BROWN painted lower section in some regions.
- Road lines: white outer lines (solid); white dashed center. Roads often narrower and more winding than France/Germany.
- Landscape: northern Po plain (flat agricultural, lat 45, Alps behind), Tuscany hills (gentle rolling, cypress trees, vineyards, lat 43), Rome/Lazio (flat/hilly, lat 42), south/Mezzogiorno (dry, brown, lat 37-40), Sicily (lat 37, dry Mediterranean), Sardinia (lat 40, rugged coastal).
- Architecture: orange/terracotta roof tiles (unique warm orange shade throughout); stone farmhouses (Tuscany: mellow yellow limestone; south: whitewashed tufa).
- CONFUSION with France: Italy has dark sign backs; French signs have lighter backs. Italian roads narrower and more winding. Italian = "Via"; French = "Rue".
- REGIONAL COORDS: Milan/Lombardy (45.5,9.2), Venice/Veneto (45.4,12.3), Florence/Tuscany (43.8,11.3), Rome/Lazio (41.9,12.5), Naples/Campania (40.8,14.3), Sicily (37.6,14.0), Sardinia (40.1,9.0).
