- Flat desert landscape; mountains only in far east (Ras al Khaimah/Gulf of Oman).
- Outer road lines yellow; center lines white or yellow. Divided roads & roundabouts dominate.
- Utility poles: dark brown wood with 3 symmetric insulators on a horizontal metal bar.
- Residential houses: light-colored stone; sidewalks often reddish stone.
- Curbs & signposts frequently feature black-and-white or black-and-yellow stripes.
- Bilingual Arabic/English signage; Arabic script highly visible on all official signs.
- Dubai-specific: blue street name signs with narrow metal borders.
