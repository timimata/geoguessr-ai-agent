- License plates: Black with center dot OR white with top blue bar.
- Poles: Round concrete (3 alternating wires), doubled concrete, or wooden A-frame (SA exclusive).
- Chevrons: White-and-red (Americas exclusive).
- Road lines: Dashed white, double yellow, or mixed.
- Km markers: White with black strip, labeled RN (national) or RP (provincial).
- Guardrails: Yellow/red rectangular reflectors.
- Vegetation/Landscape: Flat treeless grasslands (Pampas), red soil (Misiones), Caranday Wax Palm (fan leaves, single-species), Butia yatay (arching pinnate, inclined), Macaw palm (spear tip). Patagonia steppe: dry golden grass, sparse scrub, gravel roads (verify Spanish/plates to rule out USA).
- Text: Spanish only. RN/RP highway codes.
