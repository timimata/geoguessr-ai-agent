- White license plates (mix of short & long sizes); commercial vehicles use solid orange or orange-top plates.
- Ubiquitous bollards: round with two red stripes, or flat with two red reflectors on black base.
- Concrete utility poles, often featuring distinctive "ladder poles" with dense vertical indents/steps.
- Heavy "chevron spam" on road curves; concrete roads & double guardrails are common.
- Square white regulatory signs; green kilometer markers with highway shields.
- Road sign backs painted teal blue (unique in Latin America).
- One/two-way street signs featuring text inside the arrow graphic.
- Grey brick houses with metal roofs; urban areas often feature hexagonal tile pavement.
