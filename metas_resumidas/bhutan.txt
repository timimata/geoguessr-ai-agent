- Full red license plates (unique in Asia); taxis use yellow plates/roofs.
- Rural roads feature stacked stone block barriers; metal guardrails are rare.
- Traditional architecture: tin roofs with distinct wooden painted frames.
- Dzongkha script visible on signs (unique to Bhutan).
- Road markers: yellow top, white bottom; highway numbers on top.
- Signposts frequently feature black-and-white diagonal stripes.
- Two solid white edge lines only appear in western/southern Bhutan near Thimphu.
- Mountainous terrain dominates; flat agricultural valleys exclusively in the south.
- Prayer flags commonly strung near roads; thin metal poles often black-painted at base.
