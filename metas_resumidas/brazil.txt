- License plates: white with top blue stripe (post-2018 Mercosul); red for commercial. Old plates: white/green.
- Language: Portuguese (-ão, -ção, -lho, -nh suffixes). "PARE" = stop, "DEVAGAR" = slow.
- Soil: distinctive red/maroon earth (terra roxa) in south-center; NOT in Northeast (pale/sandy) or RS (dark brown).
- Poles: rectangular concrete poles with ladder-like lower segments + small top holes. Very distinctive.
- Road markings: double yellow center lines; white outer lines; white painted kerbs.
- Landscape: highly varied — Amazon (lat -4 to -10, dense jungle, poor dirt roads), Northeast caatinga (lat -5 to -12, dry sparse scrubland, pale soil, looks like US South), Southeast São Paulo/Rio (lat -22, dense urban, hilly), South (lat -26 to -30, temperate, European-looking, pine forests), Cerrado savanna (lat -12 to -20, flat brush, red soil).
- CONFUSION: Brazil→Indonesia (both tropical) — confirm with Portuguese text. Brazil→Colombia — Colombia has yellow plates + sign-back stripes.
- REGIONAL COORDS: Amazon Manaus (-3.1,-60.0), Northeast Fortaleza (-3.7,-38.5), Bahia Salvador (-12.9,-38.5), São Paulo (-23.5,-46.6), Rio de Janeiro (-22.9,-43.2), South Curitiba (-25.4,-49.3), Centro-Oeste Brasília (-15.8,-47.9), Mato Grosso (-12,-55).
