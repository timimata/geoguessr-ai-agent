- License plates: white with colorful state designs; each state has unique color scheme. "SCT" stickers on trucks. No standardized national format.
- Language: Spanish. "ALTO" = stop (not "PARE" like South America). "CEDA EL PASO" = yield. "SCT" (Secretaría de Comunicaciones y Transportes) on road signs.
- Road markings: YELLOW center lines (dashed or solid); white outer lines. Road quality ranges from good (highways) to poor (rural).
- Utility poles: concrete poles with multiple horizontal rungs; wooden poles; chaotic wiring in towns.
- Road signs: green or blue highway signs; speed in km/h. "Libramiento" = bypass. "TOPE" = speed bump (unique Mexican term on signs).
- Landscape: diverse — north (Sonoran desert, lat 29-32, dry brown cactus landscape), Gulf coast (humid tropical, lat 20), Pacific coast (dry/tropical), central plateau (lat 19-22, cities, moderate), Yucatán (flat jungle/limestone, lat 20-21), Chiapas/south (lush green mountains, lat 16-17).
- Architecture: pastel concrete houses; terracotta roof tiles; colorful painted walls; adobe in rural areas; Aztec/Maya ruins in some areas.
- CONFUSION with Guatemala/Central America: Mexico has "ALTO" stops; Central America uses "PARE". Mexico has better overall road quality in most areas.
- CONFUSION with Spain: Spain is in Europe. Mexico has yellow center lines, concrete poles, ALTO signs.
- REGIONAL COORDS: Mexico City/CDMX (19.4,-99.1), Guadalajara (20.7,-103.4), Monterrey north (25.7,-100.3), Cancun/Yucatan (21.2,-86.9), Oaxaca (17.1,-96.7), Chiapas (16.8,-93.1).
