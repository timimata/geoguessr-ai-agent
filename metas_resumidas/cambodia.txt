- License plates: White with blue text (government vehicles use green plates).
- Script: Khmer alphabet features intricate, highly curvy letters; distinct from Thai/Lao.
- Utility poles: Concrete ladder poles (ladder-like ridges) or round concrete poles with small holes.
- Road lines: Yellow center lines (dashed or solid); outer lines (if present) are solid white.
- Bollards & markers: Stone bollards with red-and-white stripes or red-top/white-base with reflector. Road markers match this stone/red-top design.
- Chevrons: Black background with yellow arrows (reverse of regional norm); often replaced by bollards.
