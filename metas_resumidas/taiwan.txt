- Traditional Chinese script (complex, multi-stroke characters) on all signage.
- Utility poles feature black & yellow diagonal stripes running ALL the way to the ground.
- Blue coordinate plaques on poles (look for "E" prefix + numbers).
- Road markings: double yellow center + single white outer lines; common variant: white outer lines only with NO center line.

- Black/yellow chevron signs; orange-hooded convex mirrors at bends/intersections.
