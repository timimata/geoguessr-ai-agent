- License plates: white with yellow EU left strip (SE). Black text, standard EU format.
- Road lines: white SHORT dashes for edge lines (very distinctive vs Finland's longer dashes); white center lines.
- Utility poles: wooden poles with painted black base; sometimes light brown concrete poles.
- Bollards: white rounded top with red reflector band; very similar to Norway/Finland but edge line dash length differs.
- Traffic signs: yellow diamond warning signs with black symbols (identical to other Scandinavian countries).
- Language: Swedish — distinct from Norwegian/Danish by words like "väg" (road), "gata" (street), "och" (and), ä/å/ö letters.
- Landscape: mostly conifer forest (pine/spruce) in north; flat agricultural land in south (Skåne); rocky archipelago coasts.
- CONFUSION with Finland: Finland has LONGER white edge dashes and Finnish language (double vowels: "aamu","talo","katu"). Sweden has SHORTER dashes and Swedish text. Norway has YELLOW center lines (Sweden = white).
- REGIONAL COORDS: Stockholm (59.3,18.1), Gothenburg (57.7,12.0), Malmö/Skåne south (55.6,13.0), Norrland north (65,17).
